Learn how to structure it

Why do the winning videos in this niche actually work?

3discover → prompts/transcript_study.md → copy 8 transcripts → paste with the included prompt

Hook type, entry point, pacing, and what none of them do. Almost nobody does this, which is part of why it's worth the twenty minutes of copying.

Free · ~20 min of manual work

Find what's coming

Cover something before the wave reaches YouTube.

7→ b → signals/reddit_signals.md

Terms climbing fast on Reddit but thin on YouTube. Treat these as leads to check, not forecasts — the idea that social chatter reliably precedes YouTube is widely repeated and, as far as I know, never actually demonstrated.

Free · no YouTube quota

Research keywords

What do people actually type into the search box?

7→ a → preset 2 then preset 5

Preset 2 covers the niche broadly, about a minute. Preset 5 is sharper and takes about two: what people search around the topics already winning. Separate files, so run both.

Free · 1–3 min depending on preset

Find a repeatable format

A series, not a one-off. At your channel size this matters more than any single video decision here.

3discover → breakouts/breakout_clusters.csv → sort by unique_channels

A cluster where seven or more separate channels succeeded is a format, not a fluke. That's the difference between one good video and a content engine — your second video benefits from the first in a way one-off topics never allow.

Free after the build

What it can't do

Worth knowing before you go looking for these.

Compare two niches head to head
Option 5 grades a niche against its own baseline, which forces demand to about 64 and supply to exactly 50. That's arithmetic, not information. Cross-niche grading exists in the code but isn't wired into the menu — for now, run option 3 on each and compare cluster strength by hand.
Tell you whether your video will work
It never sees your channel. Every number here describes the market, not your odds in it.
Judge whether a video is on-topic
There's no topical relevance filter, deliberately. Occasional off-topic leaks are the price of not silently dropping things that only look irrelevant.
Measure search demand
Scores are built from how existing videos performed. A topic nobody has covered looks identical to a topic nobody wants. Option 7 gets close from the outside, but it doesn't feed the scoring yet.
